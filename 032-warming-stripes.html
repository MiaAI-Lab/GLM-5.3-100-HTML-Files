how to read

The picture needs no numbers. That was the point.

Stripes like these were designed to be readable at a glance — on a page, a mural, a scarf. Each column is a single year's average temperature anomaly, colored by how far it sat from the 1951–1980 baseline. The palette does the arguing.

Run your cursor across the field below to inspect any year: the anomaly, and a small trace of that decade's drift. Notice how the blues thin out after 1980 like a language losing its speakers.
