TITLE: GLYPH RAIN — matrix cascade, refined
PROMPT: Design 'GLYPH RAIN', a matrix-style cascade refined into art: streams of glyphs (mixed katakana, digits, symbols) falling at varied speeds, but composed — density waves and synchronized columns create rhythm rather than noise; occasional bold 'message' moments where a phrase flickers across. Palette: near-black with phosphor green plus rare amber accents. Interactions: typing injects your characters into the rain; pointer movement attracts streams; a DENSITY slider from drizzle to deluge; a PALETTE chip set (green, amber, ice, bloom-pink); click for a radial burst that scatters columns. Iconic, disciplined, luminous.
DESCRIPTION: A disciplined Matrix-style cascade on near-black: glyph streams of mixed katakana, digits, and symbols fall at varied speeds with density waves and synchronized column rhythm rather than noise; bright lead glyphs fade into phosphor trails, with rare bold "message" phrases flickering across the field.
