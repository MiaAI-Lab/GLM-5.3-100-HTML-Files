TITLE: MIDNIGHT DINER — open all night
PROMPT: Design 'MIDNIGHT DINER', a noir landing page for a 24h diner: rain-streaked window framing the page, a buzzing red-and-blue neon OPEN sign with realistic flicker, a chrome-accented menu board with swinging items, a coffee cup with rising steam, a slowly spinning ceiling fan shadow, and a jukebox that glows. Copy: terse, warm, late-night (Pancakes. Always.). Palette: nicotine teal, ketchup red, chrome, warm tungsten. Interactions: the neon sign toggles with a pull-chain click (sign dies, page dims to moonlight, SORRY WE'RE CLOSED); the jukebox plays a lazy synth loop (WebAudio, gesture-gated) and its title card flips; hovering the menu underlines items with a greasy marker stroke; the ceiling fan speed control drags. Cinematic, cozy, smoky.
DESCRIPTION: A noir diner at 3 a.m.: a rain-streaked window backdrop (double-gradient city bokeh with diagonal streak overlays) behind a giant red-and-blue neon OPEN that buzzes with realistic double-flicker timing. The chrome-headed menu board lists terse late-night fare (Pancakes. Always.) with dotted leaders that turn into warm greasy strokes on hover; a CSS coffee cup with three rising steam wisps sits on its saucer; a repeating-conic ceiling-fan shadow rotates at adjustable speed; a glowing jukebox arch plays a lazy two-bar synth loop (triangle bass, sine melody — gesture-gated) with rotating fictional title cards. The pull-chain beside the sign kills it all: the sign dims to grey CLOSED, the window drops to moonlight, and the subline reads sorry — we're closed.
TECHNIQUES: multi-layer text-shadow neon with randomized buzz keyframes and ambient micro-flickers, pull-chain state machine re-lighting the whole scene, repeating-conic-gradient rotating fan shadow with CSS speed variables, keyframed steam wisps with blur, CSS cup-and-handle construction, WebAudio triangle/sine jukebox loop with title rotation, dotted-leader menu typography.
INTERACTION: Pull the chain to close/open the diner (full regrade + flicker re-strike); hover menu items for the warm underline; fan buttons change the shadow speed; the jukebox toggles its loop and swaps title cards.
