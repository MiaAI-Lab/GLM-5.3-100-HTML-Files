TITLE: THE BUTTON MUSEUM — please press carefully
PROMPT: Build 'THE BUTTON MUSEUM — please press carefully', a hall of microinteraction masterpieces: a curatorial dark gallery where each plinth holds ONE button, each a study — the melting chocolate press, the magnetic snap, the reluctant doorbell (long-travel spring), the confetti cannon milestone button, the haptic tick stepper, the liquid morph, the sound-and-light superbutton — each with museum label (title, year, material, 'acquired by donation'). Palette: gallery charcoal, warm spotlight pools, buttons in individual accent colors. Interactions: every button is fully pressable with its own carefully tuned animation, sound (WebAudio, gesture-gated, global mute), and consequence (some toggle, some count, one does nothing and is labeled as such — conceptual art); a PRESS COUNT odometer totals your presses; a curator's note appears after 20 presses; one hidden button unlocks a tiny fanfare. A whole museum of delight in miniature.
DESCRIPTION: A dark gallery hall with spotlight pools where eight pedestals each hold one masterwork of the button-maker's art, every one fully pressable with unique physics: the Melting Chocolate Press (border-radius melt on press), the Magnetic Snap (spring-overshoot snap), the Reluctant Doorbell of 1923 (long multi-stage travel), the Confetti Milestone (fires confetti every 5th press), the Haptic Tick Stepper (crisp 24Hz click, rotating detent glyph), the Liquid Morph (continuously morphing blob that freezes round when pressed), the golden Sound-and-Light Superbutton (rotates 180°, four-note fanfare, triple-tap encore secret), and Untitled (Button That Does Nothing) — conceptual art, pressed anyway. Museum labels carry title/year/provenance; a fixed odometer totals presses with a vault tease at 50; a curator's note slides in after 20 presses; global mute in the odometer bar.
TECHNIQUES: eight distinct button behavior systems (melt morphing, cubic-bezier overshoot, stacked box-shadow travel, CSS-custom-property confetti particles, detent glyph roulette, animation-play-state freezing, rotation reveal), WebAudio per-button voice dictionary, press-count state with per-exhibit labels and global odometer, curator-note milestone, hidden interactions (vault at 50, superbutton triple-tap encore), spotlight-pool pedestals, reduced-motion accommodations.
INTERACTION: Press every button (pointer or keyboard); each animates, sounds, and counts differently; confetti every 5th milestone press; 20 presses summon the curator; 50 total hints at the vault; three quick superbutton presses trigger the encore; sound can be muted globally.
