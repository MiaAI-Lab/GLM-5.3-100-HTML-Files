TITLE: LA PUCE — the smallest show on earth
PROMPT: Design 'LA PUCE — the smallest show on earth', a Victorian flea circus: a matchbox-scale stage with red velvet curtain, micro sawdust ring, and props (thimble, match, tiny hoop, cannon); fleas (tiny dot critters with legs) perform with physics — a flea launches from the match to dive through the hoop into the thimble, bounce on a micro trampoline, and fire from the cannon through a paper hoop; the audience is drawn as tiny silhouettes in tiered seats with occasional polite applause patters (WebAudio, gesture-gated). Palette: matchbox tan, curtain crimson, brass, ink silhouettes. Interactions: aim the cannon by dragging its barrel (power by pull distance) to hit the hoop ring; the trampoline's tension drags; TICKLE the flea for a random jump; an animated ringmaster banner announces acts; hitting all acts raises the curtain call with confetti. Miniature, absurd, meticulous.

Completing all three acts triggers the curtain call — applause patter, confetti, curtains close and reopen for an encore. Act names announce in an italic ringmaster line.
