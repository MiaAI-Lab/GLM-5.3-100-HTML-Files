TITLE: CENTO — one hundred
PROMPT: Compose 'CENTO — one hundred', the gallery's finale: a dark museum wall holding exactly 100 small living tiles in a 10×10 grid — each tile is a tiny self-contained generative sketch (a flowing gradient, an orbiting system, a breathing ring, marching dashes, pixel rain, a mini waveform…) each with its own palette, motion and micro-interaction (hovered tiles quicken; clicking a tile blooms it to a full-screen view with its title, number, and a poetic one-liner). A calm chime sounds per visit (WebAudio, gesture-gated). The grid itself breathes in a slow diagonal wave; a header sets the tone: One hundred small machines, each awake. Palette: deep charcoal wall, each tile its own curated hue family — collectively a color census of the whole gallery. Interactions: hover to energize; click to enlarge (with ESC to return); SHUFFLE reshuffles palettes; a VIEW ALL wave button sends a pulse across the entire wall; the piece is self-aware: tile 100 depicts a tiny 10×10 grid of tiles. A closing ceremony for the collection.
DESCRIPTION: The gallery's closing plate: a 10×10 wall of 100 living tiles, each a self-contained canvas sketch (flowing gradients, orbits, breathing rings, marching dashes, pixel rain, waveforms, bouncing balls, rotating trines, band sunrises, star fields) cycling through ten engines and eight palettes — collectively a color census of the whole exhibition. The wall breathes in a slow diagonal wave; hovering energizes a tile's speed; clicking blooms it full-screen with number, name (the hundred names run Flow→Haze), and a poetic one-liner (tile 100 is self-aware: it dreams of a wall of tiny machines). SHUFFLE re-deals palettes, WAVE sends a timed pulse across all hundred, and a soft sine chime greets each visit to the lightbox.
TECHNIQUES: ten parameterized generative canvas engines shared across a 100-tile field, per-tile energy state easing toward ambient rate, diagonal phase-offset breathing overlay, wave pulse sequencer with staggered timeouts, lightbox with live upscaled engine rendering, palette reshuffle, WebAudio two-partial chime (gesture-gated), ESC handling.
INTERACTION: Hover tiles to quicken them; click for the full-screen view with title and poem (ESC/click-outside to return); SHUFFLE restains the wall; WAVE pulses all 100 tiles in sequence; sound toggle for the chimes.
